Process capability, calculated instantly

Cp and Cpk tell you whether a process can meet specification — but the arithmetic is where most teams stall. This calculator takes your data and returns the indices, PPM and yield, with a plain-language read on whether the process is capable or merely marginal. Used in Six Sigma workshops for manufacturing, clinical and operations teams across Delhi NCR and India.

Your data stays on your device. The file you upload is read and calculated in your browser only — it is never sent to a server, never stored, and never seen by me. Close the tab and it is gone. I count page views with Google Analytics, never your data. Results depend on the data you enter. This is a teaching tool, not a substitute for your validated quality software or your quality system’s records.
