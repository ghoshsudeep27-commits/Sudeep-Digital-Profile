Coverage

Format and certification

Delivery

Delivered by me, not by an associate.

Four steps, no layers in between: a 30-minute discovery call, a tailored proposal with format, duration, outcomes and cost, delivery by me on-site or online, then follow-up with a summary and verifiable certificates for participants.
