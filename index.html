Mission & Approach

Profile

I combine technical AI expertise with strong interpersonal and cognitive skills to drive productivity and make a significant impact. By guiding professionals in partnering with AI, I facilitate enhanced communication and teamwork, while fostering emotional intelligence to create meaningful connections within teams.

I provide practical strategies to integrate AI tools into daily routines — saving over ten hours each week by reducing repetitive tasks. Industry 4.0

Six Sigma + AI: The Future of Manufacturing Quality Management

A workshop that fuses the disciplined rigour of Six Sigma DMAIC with the predictive power of machine learning. Participants learn how AI extends classical SPC — turning control charts into self-learning sensors that flag drift before a defect is ever produced.

The session covers AI-augmented root-cause analysis, automated Pareto discovery, and computer-vision inspection pipelines that lift process capability from 3σ to 6σ without inflating inspection headcount.

  • Format: Hands-on workshop (factory floor simulation available)
  • Tools: Control charts, Pareto, AI anomaly detection, sigma gauge
  • Outcome: A prioritised AI-Six Sigma quality roadmap
